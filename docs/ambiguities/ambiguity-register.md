## 0. How to read a row

`ID` — `AMB-<AREA>-<NNN>`, stable forever (canonical §4). `Contract pointer` — page + ماده/پیوست + بند. `Why ambiguous` — the precise defect (silence, contradiction, undefined term, two revisions of the same text). `Options` — the readings actually available, not a wish list. `Decision` — what we build, as a `DEC-*` reference; "none" means the topic is deliberately still open. `Impact` — what breaks if the decision is wrong. `Reversibility` — cost to change later: **Low** (config/flag), **Medium** (migration + workflow), **High** (schema/financial history/contract renegotiation). `Status` — `Decided` (agent baseline applied), `Escalated` (owner answer needed), `Open` (deferred to the phase that needs it), `Resolved` (owner answered), `Superseded`.

Two facts up front, because they poison any "just read the PDF" approach:

- The file contains **two revisions of pages 1–4** stacked in one text layer, so the payment plan and the support duration each exist in two versions, only one of which renders ([`../contract/contract-source.md`](../contract/contract-source.md) §3.1). Rows `AMB-CTR-005` and `AMB-CTR-006` exist for exactly that reason.
- The document is marked «پیشنویس نسخه ۱ — در انتظار توافق و امضای طرفین» with blank signature dates (page 1, page 11): `AMB-CTR-007`.

## 1. The five highest-risk rows

**`AMB-FIN-001` (custody and settlement) — highest.** Everything financial hangs off it: wallet balances, deposit payments (پیوست ۱۲ بند ۱۳), delay compensation into the buyer's wallet, refunds to شبا, supplier settlement, the bank Excel file, and the 15 % profit share base. The contract describes a wallet economy without ever saying who legally holds the money. The conservative model (platform collects → ledger → period settlement) is the only one that makes the appendices implementable, but it implies a payment-intermediary posture the client may not have licences for. This is an owner question, not an engineering one, and it must be answered before the first real rial moves.

**`AMB-ORD-005` (multi-vendor splitting).** Sub-orders are the hinge of five other clauses: per-supplier delay compensation (پیوست ۱۱ بند ۱۵), complaint freezes (پیوست ۴ بند ۱۵), per-supplier invoicing (پیوست ۱۱ بند ۱۷), settlement (پیوست ۱۳ بند ۱) and shipping (پیوست ۵ بند ۴۰). Building a single-order model and adding sub-orders later is a schema migration through every money table. Cheap now, expensive at any later date.

**`AMB-PRI-001` (stacking).** Eight reduction mechanisms can hit one line and the contract defines none of their interactions. Every implementation choice is invisible until a buyer compares two receipts. A recorded, versioned pipeline is the only defence: when the client says "the discount is wrong", the answer must be "here is the rule that fired and its version", not an argument.

**`AMB-PAY-001` / `AMB-PAY-002` (gateways).** These are the clearest contract-vs-plan conflicts in the document, with a priced penalty attached: پیوست ۱ is a numbered heading, so an unexecuted clause inside it exposes 5 % (4,500,000 T), capped at 9,000,000 overall. Unlike the other rows, this one cannot be resolved by internal decision — it needs either a written waiver or an addendum, or the feature.

**`AMB-IAM-001` (roles).** The contract's own example is self-contradictory («سطح فروشگاه نتواند بازاریاب باشد»), while پیوست ۱۲ requires simultaneous marketer and consumer activity and پیوست ۷ allows one secondary level per user. A single `role` column satisfies neither and its replacement costs a migration across policies, price lists, scopes and commission attribution.
